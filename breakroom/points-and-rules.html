Rules

Each person starts with 15 points.

Must have 100 hours to qualify for the bonus.

Loss of 15 cumulative points in a rolling 60-day period → Written Warning.

Loss of 30 cumulative points in a rolling 60-day period → 7-Day Unpaid Suspension.

Loss of 45 cumulative points in a rolling 60-day period → Termination.

Individual deductions will be assessed to the responsible employee(s) when there is an isolated incident resulting from failure to follow company procedures, negligence, or preventable employee error.

Lost equipment is an individual deduction.

Any customer issue escalated to Andrew will be entered into the claim process, regardless of reason or severity. Handled without getting to Andrew, it is not a claim.

Claim deductions include a $50 fee toward the third-party claims handler expense.

Must leave 2 weeks notice to earn the bonus.

Points reset to 15 at the start of every month. Discipline is measured on a rolling 60 days, not the calendar month. Your own points, hours and share: share.immediatemover.com
