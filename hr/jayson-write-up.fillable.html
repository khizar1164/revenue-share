Why this matters

Employee Handbook

7.1 Attendance & Punctuality

Employees are expected to be in the workplace, ready to work, at their scheduled start time and to complete their entire shift. Time off must be requested in writing, in advance, and anyone unexpectedly unable to report must notify their supervisor directly and as early as possible. A voicemail, text or email is not acceptable except in an extreme emergency.

Moving work only holds together while people, trucks, equipment, arrival windows and the jobs behind them all stay coordinated. One person's decision carries down the rest of the day.

Late — 7 min (tardy log)

Even a short delay can stop a crew leaving the shop on time. Movers work as a team, so one person being late leaves several people waiting while the customer's arrival window keeps running. That delay carries into every job scheduled afterwards and creates extra labour cost, rushed conditions and customer dissatisfaction.
